# The Last Strawberry | Fen & Nubi

## Logline
With freshly washed paws, Fen and Nubi run back to the garden for a snack, but the basket holds only one strawberry. Fen wants it all for himself, until he sees Nubi's sad face and discovers that sharing tastes even better.

## Production Notes
- **Episode 2** of Fen & Nubi, picking up right after "Wash Wash Wash Your Hands!"
- **Format**: vertical 9:16, 6 scenes × 8 s ≈ 48 seconds, one clip per scene
- **Not a song**: short spoken dialogue, one or two lines per scene, with soft background music (gentle ukulele and glockenspiel, ~100 BPM)
- **Language**: English dialogue, German subtitles burned in plus a separate `.srt`
- **Global style prompt** (same as Episode 1):
  `Pixar-style 3D cartoon for toddlers, soft rounded shapes, big glossy eyes, pastel saturated colors, warm sunny lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth motion, clean bright background, vertical 9:16, no text, no logos`
- **Voices**: bright, friendly child voices. Fen is a bit calmer; Nubi is a bit squeakier and faster
- **Camera**: gentle only; slow zooms or soft pans
- **Reuses** Fen, Nubi and the garden from Episode 1 (no new reference images needed)

## Characters
- **Fen**: cute orange baby fox, big ears, white chest, teal t-shirt with a yellow star, big curious eyes. Calmer, a little greedy at first, kind at heart.
- **Nubi**: tiny round teal baby bird, small yellow crest, yellow beak, yellow belly, cheerful. Bouncy and squeaky; her crest droops when she's sad.

---

## Scene 1: Snack Time!
**Location**: The sunny backyard garden from Episode 1: wooden sandbox, soft green lawn, flowering bushes, white picket fence. A small wicker picnic basket sits on the grass beside the sandbox.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi
**Time of Day**: Morning

Fen and Nubi run back into the garden, proudly showing their clean, shiny paws and wings. Fen spots the picnic basket and points at it excitedly.

Fen: "Clean paws… snack time!"
Nubi (squeaky): "Yay, snack time!"

**Shot Notes**: Wide shot, gentle slow push in as they reach the basket.
**Tone**: Bright, excited.

---

## Scene 2: Only One?
**Location**: The garden (same as Scene 1), beside the open picnic basket.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen opens the basket lid. Inside there is just one big, shiny red strawberry. Both peek in, then look at each other.

Nubi (surprised): "Only one strawberry?"

**Shot Notes**: Medium shot from slightly above the basket, then a gentle tilt up to their faces.
**Tone**: Curious, a little uh-oh.

---

## Scene 3: Mine!
**Location**: The garden (same as Scene 1).
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen grabs the strawberry and holds it high above his head with a cheeky grin. Beside him, Nubi's crest droops and her big eyes go sad and shiny.

Fen (cheeky): "Mine! All mine!"
Nubi (quiet, sad): "Oh…"

**Shot Notes**: Medium two-shot, static; Fen on one side holding the strawberry up, Nubi small and sad on the other.
**Tone**: Playful tension, a sad beat.

---

## Scene 4: Fen Has an Idea
**Location**: The garden (same as Scene 1).
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen looks at the strawberry, then at sad Nubi. His ears lower as he thinks. Then his face lights up. He carefully splits the strawberry into two halves and holds one out to Nubi.

Fen (warm): "Hmm… Let's share it, Nubi!"

**Shot Notes**: Slow zoom in on Fen's thoughtful face, then a gentle reveal of the two strawberry halves.
**Tone**: Thoughtful turning to warm.

---

## Scene 5: Sharing Tastes Better
**Location**: The garden (same as Scene 1), sitting together on the edge of the sandbox.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Nubi's crest pops back up. They sit side by side and munch their strawberry halves, juice on their cheeks, giggling.

Nubi (happy, squeaky): "Mmm! Sharing tastes even better!"
Fen (laughing): "Yummy!"

**Shot Notes**: Medium close-up two-shot, static, soft sunlight and floating flower petals.
**Tone**: Joyful, cozy.

---

## Scene 6: Sharing Is Caring!
**Location**: The garden (same as Scene 1), clean bright background.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen puts an arm around Nubi. Both face the camera and wave goodbye. Little heart-shaped sparkles twinkle at the edges of the frame.

Fen and Nubi (together): "Sharing is caring! Bye-bye!"

**Shot Notes**: Front-facing medium close-up, characters looking into the lens, gentle slow zoom out.
**Tone**: Warm, happy goodbye.
