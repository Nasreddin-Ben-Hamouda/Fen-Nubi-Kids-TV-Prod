# Wash Wash Wash Your Hands! | Fen & Nubi Kids Song

## Logline
After getting muddy in the sandbox, baby fox Fen and baby bird Nubi sing and dance their way through washing their hands, from wet and soapy to scrubbed, rinsed, dried and sparkling clean.

## Production Notes
- **Format**: vertical 9:16, ~50 seconds, 8 scenes, one clip per scene
- **Global style prompt** (prepend to every scene):
  `Pixar-style 3D cartoon for toddlers, soft rounded shapes, big glossy eyes, pastel saturated colors, warm sunny lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth motion, clean bright background, vertical 9:16, no text, no logos`
- **Camera**: avoid strong camera movement; slow zooms or gentle pans only
- **No text in frame**: title and subtitles are added in editing
- **Music**: ~100 BPM, simple melody with glockenspiel and ukulele
- **Voices**: bright, friendly child voices. Fen is a bit calmer; Nubi is a bit squeakier and faster
- **Chorus** (sung 3 times: Scenes 3, 6, and the closing line of Scene 8):
  > Wash, wash, wash your hands,
  > Bubbles, bubbles, everywhere!
  > Rub-a-dub, rub-a-dub, one, two, three,
  > Clean hands, happy me!

## Characters
- **Fen**: cute orange baby fox, big ears, white chest, teal t-shirt with a yellow star, big curious eyes. Playful and a little calmer of the two.
- **Nubi**: tiny round teal baby bird, small yellow crest, yellow beak, yellow belly, cheerful. Bouncy, squeaky, always fluttering.

---

## Scene 1: Muddy Fun (0–5 s)
**Location**: A sunny backyard garden with a wooden sandbox full of damp brown sand, soft green lawn, pastel flowers, and a little picket fence; bright, clean, cheerful.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi
**Time of Day**: Morning

Fen and Nubi play in the sandbox. Their paws and wings are brown and muddy. Fen laughs and holds his dirty paws up to show them off.

Nubi (funny): "Oh no, dirty hands!"

**Shot Notes**: Medium shot at the characters' eye level, gentle slow zoom in on Fen's raised paws.
**Tone**: Playful, giggly setup.

---

## Scene 2: Race to the Sink (5–10 s)
**Location**: A bright pastel bathroom with a low rounded sink, shiny chrome faucet, a mirror above it, a small step stool, a soap pump, and a fluffy towel on a hook; warm sunlight through a window.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi
**Time of Day**: Morning

Fen hops and Nubi flutters as they hurry to the sink. Ends on a close-up of the faucet.

Verse: "Mud on my paws and mud on my wings, let's wash it all off, wash everything!"

**Shot Notes**: Gentle pan following them to the sink, settling into a close-up of the faucet.
**Tone**: Energetic, bouncy.

---

## Scene 3: Bubbles Everywhere (10–18 s)
**Location**: The bathroom sink (same as Scene 2).
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen turns on the faucet and wets his paws. Soap bubbles rise up. Nubi blows a big shimmering bubble.

**Chorus** (1st time)

**Shot Notes**: Static medium shot at sink height, bubbles drifting upward through frame.
**Tone**: Joyful, sing-along.

---

## Scene 4: Scrub Scrub (18–24 s)
**Location**: The bathroom sink (same as Scene 2), background softly blurred.
**Characters**: Fen (paws only)

Extreme close-up of paws scrubbing. Colorful bubbles pop with tiny glitter sparkles.

Verse: "Front and back and in between, make them shiny, make them clean!"

**Shot Notes**: Extreme close-up, very slow push in, shallow depth of field.
**Tone**: Satisfying, rhythmic.

---

## Scene 5: Mirror Dance (24–30 s)
**Location**: The bathroom (same as Scene 2), at the mirror with the step stool.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen and Nubi sing and dance on the stool in front of the mirror while bubbles float around them.

Verse: "Count to twenty, sing along, wash away the germs so strong!"

**Shot Notes**: Medium shot, static or very gentle sway; their reflections visible in the mirror.
**Tone**: Silly, dancing fun.

---

## Scene 6: Rinse and Shine (30–38 s)
**Location**: The bathroom sink (same as Scene 2).
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Water rinses away the foam. Close-up of clean, shiny paws as little stars sparkle around them.

**Chorus** (2nd time)

**Shot Notes**: Medium shot easing into a close-up of the clean paws, slow zoom.
**Tone**: Bright, proud, sparkly.

---

## Scene 7: Fluffy Towel (38–44 s)
**Location**: The bathroom (same as Scene 2), beside the fluffy towel on its hook.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Fen and Nubi dry their paws on a fluffy towel. Nubi tickles Fen with a feather and Fen giggles.

Verse: "Dry them, dry them, soft and warm, now we're ready, safe from harm!"

**Shot Notes**: Medium two-shot, static, soft focus on the towel texture.
**Tone**: Cozy, giggly.

---

## Scene 8: Clean Hands, Happy Me! (44–50 s)
**Location**: The bathroom (same as Scene 2), clean bright background.
**Characters**: Fen, Nubi

Both hold their clean paws and wings up to the camera. Fen winks and Nubi waves. Little heart-shaped stars twinkle at the edges of the frame.

Ending: "Clean hands, happy me! Bye-bye!" + outro sound

**Shot Notes**: Front-facing medium close-up, characters looking into the lens, gentle slow zoom out.
**Tone**: Warm, happy goodbye.
